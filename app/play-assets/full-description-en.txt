Krishi Khata is a farm account book for the phone in your pocket. Income, expenses, and the labour that dominates both — recorded in seconds, kept entirely on your own device.

Built for growers in Karnataka. Kannada first, English second.

◆ WORKS WITH NO SIGNAL

No account, no sign-up, no server, no subscription. Open it in the middle of a field with the SIM out and it behaves exactly the same.

◆ RECORD A SALE IN SECONDS

Choose the crop, the variety, the quantity and the rate. Banana as G9, Mitka or Karibale. Arecanut by the kilo or the quintal. Honey by the bottle or the litre. Set each crop up once and the entry screen only ever asks what that crop actually needs.

◆ THE LABOUR LEDGER, DONE PROPERLY

This is where most farm apps give up.

• Day wages, with separate rates for men and women in a crew
• Hourly work — tractor, tiller, JCB, pump set
• Piece work such as spraying, priced per litre AFTER the job is finished
• Lump-sum jobs like coconut plucking, at whatever was agreed
• Fixed monthly workers, posted once a month

Attendance records the work. Payments record the money. An advance paid before the job settles itself against the work when the price is agreed, oldest day first. Every worker has a khata you can run a finger down.

◆ KNOW WHICH CROP IS ACTUALLY PAYING

Income against real cost per crop, including the wages that were paid for it. Cost per kilo, the rate you actually got, and profit per crop — the figure you argue with a trader about.

◆ EVERY PIECE OF LAND, SEPARATELY

Two plots or five, each shows its own profit and loss. Record which land the work was done on and the reports follow.

◆ NOT JUST THE FARM

Diesel, a car service, school fees, cloth, the electricity bill. One pocket, one book. General heads carry no crop machinery — no plot, no unit, no yield — so recording a petrol bill takes four taps.

◆ REPORTS YOU CAN HAND OVER

A proper printed statement with your farm's own letterhead: complete farm report, crop-wise profit, plot-wise profit, income and expense, wages due, day book, and a full statement for any one worker over any date range. Real PDFs, with Kannada that renders as Kannada, ready for the bank, the landlord, or the worker whose wages are on it.

◆ BACKUP WITHOUT THINKING ABOUT IT

Android saves your records to your own Google account roughly once a day and puts them back when you set up a new phone. No sign-in needed. You can also save a copy as a file and keep it in Drive, WhatsApp or a memory card.

◆ YOUR DATA IS YOURS

Krishi Khata has no server. It collects nothing, sends nothing, and shows no advertisements. Your figures never leave your phone unless you choose to share them.
